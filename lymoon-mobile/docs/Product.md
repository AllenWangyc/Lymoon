# Product Overview

Lymoon is a lightweight team shift scheduling mobile application designed for small businesses such as restaurants, cafés, and retail stores. It helps managers quickly create and publish weekly work schedules while allowing employees to easily view their assigned shifts and submit their availability.

Many small teams currently rely on spreadsheets, chat messages, or manual communication to manage schedules. This often leads to confusion, last-minute changes, and miscommunication. Lymoon aims to simplify the scheduling process by providing a clear, shared schedule system accessible from mobile devices.

The product focuses on simplicity, speed, and clarity. The first version will prioritize core scheduling functionality over advanced automation or analytics.

# Target Users

Primary Users:

- Small business managers who need to create and manage weekly work schedules.
- Team leaders responsible for coordinating employee shifts.

Secondary Users:

- Employees who need to view their assigned shifts.
- Employees who want to submit their availability to help managers schedule effectively.

Typical industries:

- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Retail stores
- Small service teams

# Core Problem

Small teams often manage scheduling using tools that are not designed for shift planning, such as spreadsheets, messaging apps, or whiteboards. This leads to several problems:

- Managers spend excessive time creating and updating schedules.
- Employees may not clearly know when they are scheduled to work.
- Availability information is difficult to collect and organize.
- Schedule updates may not reach everyone reliably.

Lymoon addresses these issues by providing a simple system where managers can create schedules and employees can easily view and communicate availability in one place.

# MVP Features

The first version of Lymoon will focus on the essential scheduling workflow.

Team Management

- Create a team.
- Invite employees to join a team using an invite link or code.

Schedule Creation

- Managers can create a weekly schedule.
- Managers can assign shifts to employees.
- Managers can edit or update shifts.

Schedule Viewing

- Employees can view their upcoming shifts.
- Employees can view the full weekly schedule.

Availability Submission

- Employees can submit their availability for upcoming weeks.
- Managers can view employee availability when creating schedules.

Notifications

- Employees receive a notification when a new schedule is published.

User Roles

- Manager: create schedules and manage the team.
- Employee: view schedules and submit availability.

# Non-MVP Features

The following features are intentionally excluded from the first version to keep the product simple:

- Automated schedule generation
- Payroll integration
- Labor cost analytics
- Multi-location management
- Advanced reporting dashboards
- Time tracking or clock-in systems
- Integration with external HR systems
- AI-assisted scheduling optimization

These features may be considered in future versions.

# User Flow

Manager Flow

1. Manager creates a team.
2. Manager invites employees using an invite link or code.
3. Employees join the team.
4. Employees submit their availability.
5. Manager creates the weekly schedule.
6. Manager assigns shifts to employees.
7. Manager publishes the schedule.
8. Employees receive notification and view their shifts.

Employee Flow

1. Employee joins a team using an invite link.
2. Employee submits availability for upcoming weeks.
3. Employee receives notification when schedule is published.
4. Employee opens the app to view their assigned shifts.

# Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if:

- Managers can create and publish a weekly schedule in less than a few minutes.
- Employees can clearly view their assigned shifts.
- Employees can submit their availability without confusion.
- Small teams can manage their schedules without relying on spreadsheets or chat messages.

Additional success indicators include:

- Managers consistently using the system to publish schedules each week.
- Employees regularly checking the app for their shifts.
- Positive feedback on ease of use and clarity of scheduling.
